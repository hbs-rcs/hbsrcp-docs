# Troubleshooting

## GPU Provisioning "Fails"

<img width="1510" height="576" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b0e7c108-cb5f-4225-90aa-445fcd828009" />

GPU instances are occasionally unavailable due to limited capacity in AWS regions. If your session shows a **Failed** status, try launching a new session with a similar configuration, or wait a few minutes and try again — availability often changes quickly. To add additional configurations to your launchers, please see our documentation [here](manageprojects.md#modify-services).
